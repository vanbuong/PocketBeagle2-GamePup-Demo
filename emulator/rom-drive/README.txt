GAMEPUP ROM INBOX
=================

1. Drag ROM files into this drive. The folders are only for organization;
   GamePup also recognizes supported files placed at the drive root.
2. Eject the GAMEPUP drive safely on your computer.
3. GamePup imports the files automatically and reconnects this drive. If
   needed, you can also open TOOLS > IMPORT USB ROMS and press A.

Supported files:
  NES                 .nes
  Game Boy / Color    .gb .gbc
  Nintendo 64         .z64 .n64 .v64
  Doom                .wad
  OLED animations     .gif
  ZIP archives        containing any of the above

The inbox keeps its copies after import. You may delete them from this drive
the next time it is mounted on your computer. ROMs are copied to GamePup's
internal game library and never added to the source repository.
