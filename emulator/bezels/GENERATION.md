# Generated system bezel assets

The `SYSTEM` bezel uses two purpose-built images generated with OpenAI's
built-in image-generation tool on July 25, 2026. The final runtime assets are
exactly 320x40 RGB888: a 320x20 top band followed by a 320x20 bottom band,
matching the ILI9341 landscape framebuffer width.

## NES prompt

Create one cohesive NES-inspired bezel image made of exactly two stacked,
extremely wide horizontal decorative bands of equal height. Use warm light gray
plastic, charcoal black, restrained red accent stripes, vents, controller-button
geometry, and subtle pixel motifs. The top band must say `NINTENDO`; the bottom
must say `ENTERTAINMENT SYSTEM`. Use large block capitals, simple bold forms,
generous safe margins, and no console photograph or silhouette.

## Game Boy Color prompt

Create one cohesive Game Boy Color-inspired bezel image made of exactly two
stacked, extremely wide horizontal decorative bands of equal height. Use deep
grape purple, soft silver-gray, black, a restrained rainbow sequence, subtle
D-pad geometry, and round button motifs. The top band must say `GAME BOY`; the
bottom must say `COLOR`. Use large block capitals, simple bold forms, generous
safe margins, and no handheld photograph or silhouette.

## Doom prompt

Create one cohesive classic Doom-inspired bezel image made of exactly two
stacked, extremely wide horizontal decorative bands of equal height. Use dark
industrial sci-fi panels, hazard stripes, rivets, stone, restrained glowing
red-orange cracks, and green status lights. The top band must say `DOOM`; the
bottom must say `SHAREWARE`. Use large block capitals, generous safe margins,
and no characters, monsters, weapons, gore, or game screenshot.

## Nintendo 64 prompt

Create one cohesive late-1990s Nintendo 64-inspired bezel image made of two
stacked, extremely wide decorative bands. Use charcoal gray faceplates, light
gray three-prong controller motifs, and restrained red, green, blue, and yellow
geometric accents. The top band must say `NINTENDO 64`; the bottom must say
`POWERED BY N64`. Use crisp pixel-art forms, large block capitals, generous safe
margins, and no characters or game screenshot.

The complete production prompts also specified a crisp polished 2D raster
style, a 16:1 overall composition for each band, high legibility after heavy
downscaling, and no other words, screenshots, perspective, or watermark.
